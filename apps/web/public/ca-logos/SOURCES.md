# CA Logo Sources

The CA marks in this folder are local copies of simple text/logo SVGs from Wikimedia Commons or Wikipedia-linked Wikimedia files. They may still be subject to trademark restrictions.

- `lets-encrypt.svg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Let%27s_Encrypt_textlogo.svg
- `google.svg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_%22G%22_logo.svg
- `aws.svg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_Web_Services_2025.svg
- `cloudflare.svg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cloudflare_Logo.svg
